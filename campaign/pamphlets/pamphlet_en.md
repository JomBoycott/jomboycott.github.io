This campaign relies strictly on **verified primary evidence**, not rumors or manufactured certainty:

   Joint winners of a **$1.2 billion** Israeli government cloud contract. Leaked tender terms forbid Google and AWS from boycotting or withholding services from defense agencies.

### Practical Alternatives: From Boycott to Digital Sovereignty
Boycotting without viable substitutes leads to paralysis. Malaysians must proactively migrate to **Free and Open-Source Software (FOSS)** and domestic sovereign infrastructure:

* **Replace Google Search:** Switch to **SearXNG** or **DuckDuckGo** (zero tracking or profiling).
* **Replace Google Drive / OneDrive:** Migrate files to **Nextcloud** (self-hosted or local cloud).
* **Replace WhatsApp:** Communicate via **Signal** or **Matrix / Element** (open encrypted protocol).
* **Replace Microsoft Office:** Use **LibreOffice** (free, open document standards, zero licensing fees).
* **European Precedent:** Germany (30,000 PCs) and France are officially ditching Microsoft & Google for Linux and Nextcloud.
* **Support Local Datacenters:** Choose Malaysian-hosted cloud providers (TM One, AIMS) to safeguard data under national sovereignty and prevent extraterritorial seizure under the US CLOUD Act.
