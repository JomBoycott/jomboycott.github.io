# WHAT DOES YOUR TECHNOLOGY HAVE TO DO WITH PALESTINE?

> *"Modern technology is no longer merely a personal convenience. It has become the backbone of 21st-century automated surveillance, military targeting, and cloud warfare."*

### 1. Your Smartphone is Wired to a Global Conflict Economy
Whenever we subscribe to cloud storage, run enterprise desktop software, host datasets on foreign hyperscalers, or navigate social media platforms, our data and subscription fees feed into corporations holding critical contracts with the Israeli state and defense apparatus.

### 2. Three Documented Realities You Need to Know:
* **Project Nimbus ($1.2 Billion):**  
  Google and Amazon Web Services (AWS) provide direct cloud computing and machine learning infrastructure to the Israeli state, with official procurement confirming the **Israeli Ministry of Defense** as a primary stakeholder. Leaked tender terms legally forbid Google and AWS from withholding service from military organs.
* **Automated Surveillance & AI Target Scoring:**  
  Across the West Bank and Gaza, civilian populations are subjected to automated biometric checkpoint systems (*Red Wolf*) and rapid intelligence processing systems (*Lavender*) that analyze billions of telecommunication points. Commercial hyperscaler clouds provide the underlying compute clusters required to store and query these massive intelligence data lakes.
* **Systemic Social Media Censorship:**  
  Meta (Facebook and Instagram) has been formally cited by independent audits (BSR) and Human Rights Watch for systematic suppression of Palestinian content—deleting on-the-ground footage, shadowbanning journalists, and complying with ~90% of extra-judicial content takedown requests by the Israeli Cyber Unit.

### 3. Our Ethical Foundation: Withholding Cooperation in Harm
Our stance is grounded in universal justice and Islamic ethics:
> **"Cooperate in righteousness and piety, but do not cooperate in sin and transgression."**  
> *(Qur'an 5:2)*

Resisting corporate complicity is not an act of ethnic or religious animosity; it is an ethical imperative demanding that technology serve human dignity rather than state oppression.

# WHAT CAN MALAYSIANS DO?

> *"Boycott without alternatives breeds helplessness. Boycott with alternatives builds sovereignty."*

The purpose of this campaign is not to demand that Malaysians disconnect from the modern world, but to embark on a structured, realistic transition toward **Malaysian Digital Sovereignty** and **Free and Open-Source Software (FOSS)**.

### A 4-Step Practical Action Plan for You & Your Organisation:

#### Step 1: Liberate Your Search & Browsing
* Stop allowing Google to track and monetize every query you enter.
* Install **Mozilla Firefox** or **Brave**.
* Set **SearXNG** or **DuckDuckGo** as your default, privacy-preserving search engine.

#### Step 2: Protect Your Communications
* Move sensitive personal, family, and business discussions off Meta's surveillance advertising platforms.
* Use **Signal** for state-of-the-art encrypted direct messaging.
* Deploy open **Matrix / Element** networks for company and community teamwork.

#### Step 3: Secure Your Documents & School Work
* Replace Microsoft Office with **LibreOffice** — completely free, open-standard (ODF), and free of recurring USD licensing charges.
* Store your archives and collaborate on files using sovereign **Nextcloud** instances hosted locally.

#### Step 4: Demand Sovereign National Infrastructure
* Malaysia launched the National Cloud Computing Policy (NCCP) in August 2025.
* Citizens and businesses must advocate for public sector agencies and universities to avoid total vendor lock-in with foreign hyperscalers subject to the extraterritorial reach of the US CLOUD Act.
* Follow Europe's lead: German states (30,000 PCs to Linux) and French ministries have banned Google & Microsoft 365 in schools to reclaim digital sovereignty!
* Champion homegrown, world-class datacenters (such as TM One and AIMS) and invest in Malaysian software engineering talent.
