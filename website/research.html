Disiplin Penyelidikan & Taksonomi Fakta Research Discipline & Evidentiary Taxonomy

Prinsip Emas: Jangan mereka kepastian (Do not manufacture certainty). Golden Principle: Do not manufacture certainty. Maintain strict legal distinctions across all claims.

5 Kategori Pengelasan Fakta 5-Tier Evidentiary Classification System

Kategori A — Fakta Sah (Verified Fact) Category A — Verified Fact

Disokong terus oleh dokumen rasmi kerajaan, keputusan mahkamah antarabangsa (ICJ/ICC), pemfailan korporat SEC, atau kontrak perolehan yang sah. Directly established by primary government gazettes, international court rulings (ICJ/ICC), SEC regulatory filings, or unredacted tender documents.

Kategori B — Laporan Disokong Kuat (Strongly Supported Reporting) Category B — Strongly Supported Reporting

Disokong oleh pelbagai sumber kewartawanan penyiasatan bebas yang berwibawa (The Guardian, Reuters, AP, Time, Financial Times, Wired). Corroborated by multiple credible, tier-one independent investigative outlets (e.g. The Guardian, Reuters, AP, Time, Financial Times, Wired).

Kategori C — Dakwaan (Allegation) Category C — Plausible Allegation

Organisasi hak asasi membuat dakwaan yang munasabah, tetapi fakta dasar masih dipertikaikan atau belum lengkap sepenuhnya. Documented complaints filed by credible civil society groups where evidentiary confirmation remains actively disputed or incomplete.

Kategori D — Tafsiran Analitis (Interpretation) Category D — Analytical Interpretation

Analisis logik atau kesimpulan teknikal yang diterbitkan daripada fakta-fakta yang telah mantap. Technical deductive assessments and policy inferences logically derived from verified baseline facts.

Kategori E — Pendapat Kempen (Campaign Opinion) Category E — Campaign Advocacy Position

Kesimpulan etika atau pendirian politik advokasi kempen. Tidak boleh sekali-kali dipersembahkan sebagai fakta undang-undang mutlak. Ethical recommendations, religious judgments, or civic policy stances clearly delineated from empirical legal determinations.

5 Soalan Penyelidikan Teras 5 Core Investigatory Questions

Bagi setiap syarikat yang disiasat, penyelidik kempen menjawab lima soalan secara berasingan: For every corporation examined, campaign researchers independently address five distinct questions:

  1. Apakah yang sebenarnya terbukti? (What is actually proven?) What is actually proven by primary documents?
  2. Apakah yang didakwa? (What is alleged?) What is alleged by rights organizations and workers?
  3. Apakah yang dikatakan oleh syarikat? (What does the company say?) What is the company's official public response and legal defense?
  4. Apakah yang dikatakan oleh penyiasat bebas? (What do independent investigators say?) What have independent audits and investigative reporters confirmed?
  5. Apakah yang masih belum diketahui? (What remains unknown?) What remains classified, unredacted, or scientifically unproven?
