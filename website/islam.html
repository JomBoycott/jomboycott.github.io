Asas Etika Islam: Keadilan Tanpa Penindasan Islamic Ethical Foundations: Justice Against Oppression

Kempen ini berakar umbi daripada tradisi keilmuan Islam muktabar—menuntut keadilan, membela orang yang tertindas, dan mengharamkan kerjasama dalam dosa dan kezaliman. This campaign is deeply grounded in classical Islamic jurisprudence—demanding justice, defending the oppressed (al-mustad'afin), and strictly prohibiting complicity in sin and aggression.

وَتَعَاوَنُوا عَلَى الْبِرِّ وَالتَّقْوَىٰ ۖ وَلَا تَعَاوَنُوا عَلَى الْإِثْمِ وَالْعُدْوَانِ ۚ وَاتَّقُوا اللَّهَ ۖ إِنَّ اللَّهَ شَدِيدُ الْعِقَابِ

Surah Al-Ma'idah (5:2) — Larangan Bersubahat Dalam Pencerobohan Surah Al-Ma'idah (5:2) — Prohibition of Complicity in Sin & Aggression

"Dan hendaklah kamu bertolong-tolongan untuk membuat kebajikan dan bertakwa, dan janganlah kamu bertolong-tolongan pada melakukan dosa dan pencerobohan; dan bertakwalah kepada Allah, sesungguhnya Allah amat berat azab seksa-Nya." "And cooperate in righteousness and piety, but do not cooperate in sin and aggression. And fear Allah; indeed, Allah is severe in penalty."

Huraian Tafsir Al-Qurtubi (Al-Jami' li-Ahkam al-Qur'an): Exegesis by Imam Al-Qurtubi (Al-Jami' li-Ahkam al-Qur'an):

Imam Al-Qurtubi merumuskan bahawa ayat ini mengharamkan sebarang bentuk bantuan kepada perkara mungkar, termasuk menjual anggur kepada pihak yang akan memprosesnya menjadi arak, atau menjual besi dan senjata kepada perompak. Dalam konteks moden, membekalkan infrastruktur awan dan pangkalan data kepada kementerian pertahanan yang sedang menceroboh hak asasi manusia adalah termasuk dalam al-i'anah 'ala al-'udwan (membantu pencerobohan) yang diharamkan syarak. Imam Al-Qurtubi established that this verse categorically forbids facilitating wrongdoing in any commercial transaction—such as selling grapes to a winemaker or forging iron weapons for bandits. In contemporary terms, providing enterprise cloud compute and AI targeting pipelines to defense organs violating international human rights falls squarely under al-i'anah 'ala al-'udwan (material assistance to aggression), which is strictly unlawful (haram).

«انْصُرْ أَخَاكَ ظَالِمًا أَوْ مَظْلُومًا» فَقَالَ رَجُلٌ: يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ أَنْصُرُهُ إِذَا كَانَ مَظْلُومًا، أَفَرَأَيْتَ إِذَا كَانَ ظَالِمًا كَيْفَ أَنْصُرُهُ؟ قَالَ: «تَحْجُزُهُ ـ أَوْ تَمْنَعُهُ ـ مِنَ الظُّلْمِ فَإِنَّ ذَلِكَ نَصْرُهُ»

Sahih al-Bukhari 2444 — Mencegah Orang Zalim Adalah Cara Membantunya Sahih al-Bukhari 2444 — Restraining the Oppressor is How You Help Him

Rasulullah ﷺ bersabda: "Tolonglah saudaramu, sama ada dia seorang yang zalim ataupun yang dizalimi." Seorang lelaki bertanya: "Bagaimanakah cara aku menolongnya jika dia zalim?" Baginda bersabda: "Kamu menghalangnya daripada melakukan kezaliman, sesungguhnya demikian itulah cara menolongnya." The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "Help your brother whether he is an oppressor or is being oppressed." A man asked: "O Messenger of Allah, I help him when he is oppressed, but how can I help him when he is an oppressor?" The Prophet ﷺ replied: "By restraining him—or preventing him—from committing oppression, for that is indeed helping him."

Hadis ini mengubah paradigma kempen: bantahan kita bukan berasaskan kebencian kepada mana-mana kaum atau pekerja teknologi, tetapi satu usaha moral untuk menyekat tangan pihak yang menzalimi agar jenayah kemanusiaan dapat dihentikan. This prophetic decree reframes tech accountability: our boycott is never motivated by animus toward any ethnic community or individual tech worker, but stands as a moral duty to restrain the apparatus of oppression and halt violations of human life and dignity.

Keadilan Sejagat & Penolakan Mutlak Terhadap Perkauman Universal Justice & Absolute Rejection of Antisemitism and Racism

Islam melarang kebencian terhadap sesuatu kaum mendorong kita melakukan ketidakadilan (Surah Al-Ma'idah 5:8). Kempen ini mengekalkan garis pemisah yang sangat tegas: Islam explicitly commands that hatred of a people must never cause deviation from justice (Surah Al-Ma'idah 5:8). Our campaign maintains strict, non-negotiable boundaries:

✅ Sasaran Sah Akauntabiliti: ✅ Legitimate Targets of Accountability:
  • Kontrak pertahanan dan awan ketenteraan (Project Nimbus). Military and defense cloud contracts (e.g. Project Nimbus).
  • Pelanggaran undang-undang antarabangsa dan perintah Mahkamah Keadilan Antarabangsa (ICJ). Violations of international humanitarian law and ICJ provisional measures.
  • Sistem pengawasan biometrik dan penyasaran AI tanpa kawalan etika. Unchecked mass biometric surveillance and military AI targeting pipelines.
  • Penapisan ucapan aman dan suara kebebasan di platform media sosial. Systemic platform censorship suppressing peaceful speech and documenting atrocities.
❌ Yang Ditolak Keras: ❌ Strictly Prohibited & Condemned:
  • Antisemitisme, Islamofobia, dan sebarang bentuk kebencian agama. Antisemitism, Islamophobia, and all forms of religious animus.
  • Diskriminasi berasaskan bangsa, etnik, atau kewarganegaraan. Discrimination based on race, ethnicity, or nationality.
  • Serangan peribadi atau gangguan terhadap jurutera dan pekerja bawahan korporat. Personal harassment of individual tech engineers or frontline corporate workers.
  • Penyebaran khabar angin, fitnah, dan fabrikasi maklumat tanpa dokumen sah. Spreading unverified rumors, conspiracy theories, or manufactured certainty.
