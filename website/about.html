Misi & Falsafah Kempen Mission & Philosophy of the Campaign

Gerakan awam sumber terbuka yang menggabungkan etika Islam, ketelitian bukti undang-undang antarabangsa, dan kemandirian teknologi Malaysia. An open-source public movement integrating Islamic ethics, international legal evidence, and Malaysian technological independence.

6 Tunjang Falsafah Kempen (6 Pillars) 6 Core Philosophical Pillars

1. Keadilan 1. Justice ('Adl)

Tidak menyokong atau bersubahat dengan sebarang bentuk kezaliman dan penindasan kemanusiaan. Refusing complicity in all forms of structural oppression, human rights abuses, and unlawful aggression.

2. Disiplin Bukti 2. Evidentiary Discipline

Menolak rekaan kepastian. Hanya menyandarkan dakwaan kepada dokumen sah dan kewartawanan bertaraf dunia. Rejecting manufactured certainty. Anchoring every claim in primary tenders, corporate disclosures, and court records.

3. Pilihan Pengguna 3. Practical Agency

Memberi rakyat alternatif praktikal. Boikot tanpa alternatif melumpuhkan; boikot dengan alternatif memerdekakan. Empowering citizens with viable substitutes. Boycott without alternatives paralyzes; boycott with alternatives liberates.

4. Kedaulatan Digital 4. Digital Sovereignty

Mengurangkan kebergantungan melampau kepada monopoli teknologi asing dan melindungi data negara daripada Akta CLOUD AS. Reducing critical dependency on foreign monopolies and shielding national data from extraterritorial US CLOUD Act warrants.

5. Sumber Terbuka (FOSS) 5. Open Source (FOSS)

Menyokong perisian terbuka yang telus, boleh diaudit, dan tidak mengunci pengguna dalam lesen proprietari mahal. Advancing transparent, auditable, community-governed software stacks free from proprietary vendor capture.

6. Keamanan & Kemanusiaan 6. Universal Ethics

Menolak sekeras-kerasnya antisemitisme dan perkauman. Membezakan secara tegas antara kerajaan dengan rakyat, tentera dengan etnik. Strictly rejecting antisemitism, racism, and collective guilt. Differentiating states from citizens, militaries from ethnic communities.

Ujian Adversarial Wajib (Adversarial Scrutiny) Mandatory Adversarial Scrutiny

Sebelum sesuatu penerbitan diluluskan, penyelidik kempen wajib bertanyakan soalan ini: Before any finding is published, campaign researchers must rigorously ask:

"Sekiranya peguam Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Meta, jurucakap rasmi kerajaan Israel, jurutera perisian, dan ulama Islam membaca halaman ini, apakah yang boleh mereka pertikaikan secara sah?" "If attorneys representing Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Meta, Israeli government spokespeople, corporate software architects, and Islamic legal jurists read this page, what could they legally or factually refute?"

Kelemahan atau kekaburan yang ditemui wajib diperbetulkan serta-merta. Kekuatan terbesar kempen ini adalah hakikat bahawa peguam korporat tidak boleh menyamannya kerana setiap dakwaan disandarkan kepada rekod dokumen rasmi awam mereka sendiri. Any ambiguities or unsubstantiated claims discovered must be corrected immediately. The greatest strength of this campaign is legal and factual resilience: corporate lawyers cannot dismiss our findings because every claim is anchored in their own official filings.
