Soalan Lazim & Hujah Lawan (FAQ) Frequently Asked Questions & Adversarial Responses

Menjawab persoalan kritikal daripada peguam, jurutera, pembuat dasar, dan orang awam. Direct, unevasive answers to critical objections from corporate attorneys, cloud engineers, policymakers, and citizens.

1. Adakah kempen ini mendakwa jurutera Google/Meta menulis sendiri kod pengeboman tentera? 1. Does this campaign allege that Google or Meta engineers write military bombing algorithms?

Tidak sama sekali. Disiplin penyelidikan kami membezakan secara tegas antara kod algoritma ketenteraan (seperti Lavender dan The Gospel) yang dibangunkan sendiri oleh unit tentera Unit 8200, dengan infrastruktur awan komersial (seperti Project Nimbus) yang membekalkan kluster pengkomputeran, storan pangkalan data mega, dan keupayaan pembelajaran mesin untuk menyokong operasi data tentera tersebut. Categorically not. Our evidentiary discipline rigorously differentiates between proprietary military targeting algorithms (such as Lavender and The Gospel, engineered in-house by military Unit 8200) and commercial hyperscaler cloud infrastructure (such as Project Nimbus, which provisions compute instances, object storage, and GPU clusters utilized by defense agencies).

2. Mengapa Microsoft dinilai secara berbeza berbanding Google dan Amazon? 2. Why is Microsoft evaluated differently from Google and Amazon?

Microsoft kalah dalam bidaan Project Nimbus kepada Google dan AWS pada 2021, jadi ia tidak terikat dengan klausa 'tiada boikot' Nimbus. Walaupun Microsoft membekalkan sistem perisian perusahaan kepada kementerian pertahanan Israel, di bawah desakan bantahan pekerja dalaman (No Azure for Apartheid), pada September 2025 Microsoft dilaporkan menamatkan akses tentera kepada teknologi pengawasan tertentu di Gaza dan memperketatkan semakan hak asasi manusia. Kejujuran intelektual menuntut kita mencatatkan fakta ini. Microsoft lost the Project Nimbus tender to Google and AWS in 2021, and is therefore not bound by Nimbus's rigid 'no-boycott' clause. While Microsoft provides enterprise licensing and Azure services to defense agencies, facing internal employee resistance (No Azure for Apartheid), in September 2025 Microsoft terminated military access to specific mass surveillance technologies in Gaza and the West Bank and instituted external human rights reviews. Intellectual integrity requires documenting this crucial distinction.

3. Adakah kempen ini mengandungi unsur antisemitisme atau kebencian agama? 3. Does this campaign contain antisemitism or religious hatred?

Sama sekali tidak. Islam mengharamkan kezaliman terhadap penganut agama Yahudi (Surah Al-Ma'idah 5:8). Kempen ini secara tegas membezakan antara: (1) penganut Yahudi dengan kerajaan Israel; (2) rakyat awam dengan tentera; dan (3) perbadanan teknologi dengan pekerjanya (ramai pekerja berani membantah dan dipecat). Bantahan kami adalah terhadap kontrak komersial yang membolehkan penindasan hak asasi manusia berlaku. Absolutely not. Islamic law strictly forbids prejudice or injustice against Jewish people (Surah Al-Ma'idah 5:8). Our campaign maintains unambiguous distinctions between: (1) Jewish people and the Israeli government; (2) civilians and military forces; and (3) corporate entities and individual workers (many of whom courageously protested and were dismissed). Our campaign targets corporate complicity in documented human rights violations.

4. Bolehkah ekonomi dan pentadbiran Malaysia berfungsi tanpa Big Tech? 4. Can Malaysia's economy and administration function without American Big Tech?

Boleh, melalui pendekatan hibrid-berdaulat yang berperingkat. Siasatan majalah Wired (Jun 2026) membuktikan bahawa kerajaan Eropah sedang melakukannya: negeri Schleswig-Holstein di Jerman kini memindahkan 30,000 PC pentadbiran ke Linux dan LibreOffice, manakala Perancis melarang Google & Microsoft di sekolah demi kedaulatan data. Malaysia mempunyai pusat data domestik Tier III (TM One, AIMS) dan dasar awan NCCP 2025 yang memberi mandat mengurangkan kebergantungan kepada satu vendor asing sahaja. Yes, through a progressive, sovereign hybrid approach. An investigation by Wired (June 2026) proves European administrations are actively doing so: the German state of Schleswig-Holstein is migrating 30,000 workstations to Linux and LibreOffice, while France has banned Google Workspace and MS365 in public schools. Malaysia possesses certified Tier III facilities (TM One, AIMS) and an official National Cloud Computing Policy (NCCP 2025) mandating vendor diversification.

5. Bagaimana dengan sekolah dan pelajar yang menggunakan Google Classroom? 5. What about schools and students currently reliant on Google Classroom?

Kempen ini mencadangkan Laluan Migrasi 6-Tahap. Sekolah tidak perlu memotong capaian serta-merta, tetapi Kementerian Pendidikan boleh memulakan projek perintis menggunakan sistem pengurusan pembelajaran sumber terbuka (seperti Moodle) yang dihoskan di pelayan awan kerajaan tempatan, sambil mengajar pelajar sistem operasi Linux untuk melahirkan generasi jurutera yang celik kod sumber. The campaign advocates a phased 6-Level Migration Roadmap. Schools should not disrupt active semesters overnight, but the Ministry of Education should pilot open-source Learning Management Systems (such as Moodle) on sovereign domestic government clouds, while introducing Linux to foster a generation of engineers fluent in open systems.
