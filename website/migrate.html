Laluan 6-Tahap Keluar Dari Big Tech The 6-Level Exit Big Tech Roadmap

Jangan memadam semua akaun dalam satu hari. Ambil langkah berperingkat yang mampan dan praktikal untuk anda, keluarga, dan syarikat anda. Do not attempt to delete everything overnight. Take progressive, sustainable steps for lasting personal, organizational, and national digital independence.

Tahap 1 • Minggu 1 Level 1 • Week 1

Kesedaran & Audit Digital Digital Footprint & Dependency Audit

Kenal pasti semua perkhidmatan awan yang anda gunakan. Muat turun sandaran arkib fail anda menggunakan Google Takeout atau ciri eksport data Microsoft/Meta. Map all your cloud dependencies. Download archive backups of your photos, documents, and contacts via Google Takeout or Microsoft/Meta data export tools.

Tahap 2 • Minggu 2–4 Level 2 • Weeks 2–4

Gantikan Satu Perkhidmatan Mudah Low-Friction Everyday Consumer Swaps

Beralih daripada Google Chrome kepada Mozilla Firefox atau Brave. Tukar enjin carian kepada SearXNG atau DuckDuckGo. Mula menggunakan Signal bersama rakan terdekat. Switch from Google Chrome to Mozilla Firefox or Brave. Change default search to SearXNG or DuckDuckGo. Adopt Signal with close family and colleagues.

Tahap 3 • Bulan 2–3 Level 3 • Months 2–3

Gantikan Suite Pejabat & Storan Fail Productivity Suite & Personal Cloud Storage

Pasang LibreOffice di komputer peribadi anda (bebas yuran lesen Microsoft Office). Alihkan fail peribadi dari Google Drive kepada storan peribadi Nextcloud atau pemacu luaran. Install LibreOffice on personal computers (100% free of Microsoft Office USD subscriptions). Migrate Google Drive files to a self-hosted or local Nextcloud instance or encrypted local drive.

Tahap 4 • Bulan 3–6 Level 4 • Months 3–6

Saluran Komunikasi Bebas & Mesyuarat Decentralized Collaboration & Video Meetings

Gunakan Jitsi Meet untuk mesyuarat video tanpa perlu log masuk. Pindahkan kumpulan perbincangan kelab atau komuniti dari WhatsApp ke Matrix / Element. Host meetings with Jitsi Meet without requiring proprietary logins. Move community or team discussion rooms from WhatsApp to federated Matrix / Element.

Tahap 5 • Bulan 6–12 Level 5 • Months 6–12

Migrasi Syarikat PKS, Sekolah & NGO SME, School & Organizational Infrastructure

Pasang pelayan e-mel Mailcow pada pelayan tempatan (jimat sehingga 70% perbelanjaan IT berbanding Google Workspace). Jalankan model AI tempatan melalui Ollama untuk menjaga kerahsiaan data pelanggan. Deploy self-hosted corporate mail with Mailcow (saving up to 70% in foreign exchange IT licensing fees). Run private on-premise AI models via Ollama to safeguard client data confidentiality.

Tahap 6 • Jangka Panjang Level 6 • Long-Term

Infrastruktur Berdaulat Peringkat Nasional National Sovereign Infrastructure & Public Policy

Mendesak kerajaan melaksanakan Dasar Pengkomputeran Awan Negara (NCCP) berasaskan piawaian terbuka (OpenStack, Kubernetes) di pusat data domestik (TM One, AIMS) bagi menjamin kemerdekaan siber Malaysia. Advocate for government adoption of open-standard cloud infrastructure (OpenStack, Kubernetes) hosted in Malaysian Tier-III facilities (TM One, AIMS) under the National Cloud Computing Policy.
