Dosier Penyelidikan 4 Syarikat Gergasi Dossiers on 4 Tech Corporations

Penyelidikan terperinci merangkumi kontrak Nimbus, pusat data, sekatan pengawasan 2025, dan penyederhanaan kandungan. In-depth dossiers covering Nimbus, regional datacenters, 2025 surveillance restrictions, and platform policies.

Google / Alphabet Amazon / AWS Microsoft Meta Platforms

Google / Alphabet

Project Nimbus $1.2B Project Nimbus $1.2B Award

Peranan: Pembekal infrastruktur awan teras (Google Cloud Platform), model pembelajaran mesin (Vertex AI / AutoML), dan zon pusat data Tel Aviv (me-west1). Role: Core cloud infrastructure provider (Google Cloud Platform), machine learning models (Vertex AI / AutoML), and Tel Aviv datacenter region (me-west1).

Bukti Disahkan Utama: Key Verified Evidence:

  • Pemenang bersama Project Nimbus bernilai $1.2 bilion pada April 2021 bersama AWS. Joint winner of the $1.2 billion Project Nimbus tender in April 2021 alongside AWS.
  • Siasatan Time Magazine (April 2024) mendedahkan Kementerian Pertahanan Israel mempunyai kontrak perundingan dan saluran komputasi terus ke Google Cloud Platform semasa perang di Gaza. Time Magazine investigation (April 2024) revealed the Israeli Ministry of Defense has direct consulting contracts and a dedicated landing zone into Google Cloud Platform during the Gaza war.
  • Lebih 50 jurutera dan kakitangan Google dipecat di California dan New York kerana menganjurkan bantahan aman menolak Nimbus. Over 50 Google engineers and workers were fired in California and New York for organizing peaceful sit-in protests against Nimbus.

Maklum Balas Syarikat: Google menyatakan beban kerja Nimbus adalah untuk kementerian awam (kesihatan, pengangkutan), namun syarat tender rasmi melarang Google daripada menyekat perkhidmatan kepada kementerian pertahanan. Corporate Response: Google maintains Nimbus serves civilian ministries (health, transport), yet official tender terms contractually bar Google from denying services to defense bodies.

Amazon / AWS

Rantau Tel Aviv $7.2B Tel Aviv Region $7.2B

Peranan: Pembekal awan terbesar dunia yang membekalkan pangkalan data, storan objek (S3), dan pengkomputeran berprestasi tinggi untuk agensi keselamatan Israel. Role: Global cloud leader providing database clusters, object storage (S3), and high-performance compute to Israeli security agencies.

Bukti Disahkan Utama: Key Verified Evidence:

  • Pemenang bersama Nimbus membekalkan perkhidmatan awan IaaS dan analitik keselamatan kepada polis dan tentera. Co-winner of Nimbus supplying IaaS compute and security analytics to police and military authorities.
  • Melancarkan AWS Israel Region (il-central-1) pada Ogos 2023 dengan komitmen pelaburan $7.2 bilion hingga 2037. Launched the AWS Israel Region (il-central-1) in August 2023 committing $7.2 billion in long-term infrastructure through 2037.
  • Klausa tender mengikat AWS untuk tidak mematuhi sebarang boikot atau sekatan terhadap kementerian pertahanan Israel. Contractual tender clauses bind AWS to never comply with boycott pressures or withhold compute from Israeli defense organs.

Kaitan Malaysia: AWS melancarkan AWS Malaysia Region (ap-southeast-5) pada 2024 dengan pelaburan $6.2B, memegang Perjanjian Rangka Kerja Awan (CFA) kerajaan Malaysia. Malaysian Relevance: AWS launched the AWS Malaysia Region (ap-southeast-5) in 2024 with a $6.2B investment, holding a Malaysian Cloud Framework Agreement (CFA).

Microsoft (Nuansa & Sekatan 2025) Microsoft (Nuance & 2025 Sanctions)

Sekatan Pengawasan Sept 2025 Sept 2025 Surveillance Sanctions

Peranan: Pembekal sistem perusahaan, Windows, dan Azure Cloud kepada sektor pentadbiran dan keselamatan, dengan pusat R&D utama di Herzliya. Role: Supplier of enterprise software, Windows, and Azure Cloud to government and defense sectors, with major R&D hubs in Herzliya.

Nuansa Kritikal & Rekod Terbukti: Critical Nuance & Verified Record:

  • Microsoft BUKAN pemenang Project Nimbus (kalah bidaan kepada Google/AWS pada 2021). Microsoft was NOT awarded Project Nimbus (lost the public tender to Google/AWS in 2021).
  • Menghadapi bantahan pekerja No Azure for Apartheid, pada September 2025 Microsoft menamatkan capaian tentera Israel kepada teknologi pengawasan tertentu di Gaza dan Tebing Barat berasaskan Polisi Penggunaan Boleh Diterima. Facing internal employee dissent (No Azure for Apartheid), in September 2025 Microsoft terminated Israeli military access to specific mass surveillance technologies in Gaza and the West Bank under its Acceptable Use Policy.
  • Melancarkan audit hak asasi manusia bebas dan mengetatkan semakan kontrak keselamatan negara pada Jun 2026. Commissioned an independent human rights review and tightened national security contract screening guidelines in June 2026.

Integriti Penyelidikan: Menyatakan kedua-dua aspek (kontrak pertahanan sedia ada dan sekatan yang diambil) adalah asas mengekalkan kebenaran berpandukan Surah An-Nisa' 4:135. Research Integrity: Acknowledging both dimensions (defense contracts and corrective sanctions) upholds truthful witnessing per Surah An-Nisa' 4:135.

Meta Platforms (Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp)

Audit Bebas BSR & HRW BSR & HRW Audits

Peranan: Penjaga pintu wacana sosial dan komunikasi global (bukan pembekal awan pelayan ketenteraan). Role: Gatekeeper of global social discourse and messaging infrastructure (not a military datacenter provider).

Bukti Disahkan Utama: Key Verified Evidence:

  • Audit bebas BSR (2022) mengesahkan tindakan penapisan Meta memberi kesan buruk kepada hak bersuara rakyat Palestin. Independent BSR Human Rights Audit (2022) confirmed Meta moderation practices caused adverse human rights impacts on Palestinian users' free expression.
  • Laporan Human Rights Watch (2023) mendokumenkan lebih 1,050 kes penapisan dan shadowbanning akaun wartawan dan aktivis pro-Palestin. Human Rights Watch report (2023) documented over 1,050 cases of systemic censorship and shadowbanning targeting Palestinian journalists and activists.
  • Lembaga Pengawasan Meta membatalkan larangan menyeluruh ke atas perkataan Arab "Shaheed" pada Julai 2024 dan mengesahkan slogan "From the River to the Sea" pada September 2024. Meta Oversight Board overturned blanket bans on the Arabic word "Shaheed" (July 2024) and upheld the phrase "From the River to the Sea" (September 2024).
  • Meta mematuhi anggaran 85-90% permohonan pemadaman sukarela oleh Unit Siber Israel tanpa waran mahkamah. Meta complied with an estimated 85-90% of voluntary takedown requests from the Israeli Cyber Unit without judicial warrants.
