Kedaulatan Digital Malaysia: Mengapa Ia Kritikal? Malaysian Digital Sovereignty: Why It Matters

Bolehkah Malaysia membina ekosistem digital yang maju dan selamat tanpa bergantung 100% kepada empat gergasi teknologi asing? Can Malaysia foster a resilient digital economy without total dependency on four foreign tech monopolies?

⚖️

Akta CLOUD AS (2018) US CLOUD Act (2018)

Undang-undang AS memberi kuasa kepada mahkamah Washington untuk menuntut data pengguna daripada syarikat AS (Google, AWS, Microsoft), walaupun pelayan tersebut berada di Cyberjaya atau Johor. Data warganegara Malaysia terdedah kepada bidang kuasa asing. US law empowers Washington courts to compel US companies (Google, AWS, Microsoft) to produce data regardless of whether servers reside in Cyberjaya or Johor. Malaysian citizens' data remains subject to extraterritorial warrants.

💸

Penirisan Aliran Tunai Outflow of Capital

Setiap tahun, berbilion Ringgit mengalir keluar dari ekonomi negara untuk yuran pelesenan perisian proprietari dan langganan awan USD. Wang ini sepatutnya membina keupayaan jurutera perisian dan syarikat teknologi tempatan. Billions of Ringgit leak out of the national economy every year in recurring USD subscriptions and proprietary software licensing fees. These public funds should instead build local engineering capacity and homegrown tech firms.

🔇

Penapisan Ekstrateritorial Extraterritorial Censorship

Ucapan dan hantaran rasmi pemimpin tertinggi negara (termasuk Perdana Menteri) pernah dipadam oleh Meta secara sepihak. Monopoli platform komunikasi asing merapuhkan kebebasan wacana kedaulatan negara. Official statements by Malaysia's national leadership (including the Prime Minister) have repeatedly been unilaterally deleted by Meta. Reliance on foreign gatekeeper platforms undermines national communicative sovereignty.

Keupayaan Pusat Data & Prasarana Tempatan Domestic Datacenter & Network Capabilities

Malaysia memiliki prasarana telekomunikasi dan pusat data domestik yang kukuh untuk menyokong kedaulatan data: Malaysia possesses robust, world-class telecommunications and datacenter infrastructure to anchor genuine data sovereignty:

TM One (Telekom Malaysia)

Pusat data Tier III KVDC (Klang Valley Data Centre) dan CJDC (Core Data Centre Cyberjaya) yang dihubungkan dengan rangkaian gentian optik nasional. Menjadi penyedia tempatan tunggal dalam Perjanjian Rangka Kerja Awan (CFA). Tier III Klang Valley Data Centre (KVDC) and Cyberjaya Core Data Centre (CJDC) connected by Malaysia's national fiber backbone. Serving as the sole domestic Cloud Service Provider under the government's CFA.

AIMS Data Centre (Time dotCom)

Hab interkoneksi telekomunikasi paling neutral dan sibuk di Malaysia, menyokong pertukaran trafik internet kebangsaan (MyIX) dengan kependaman sangat rendah. Malaysia's most interconnected, carrier-neutral interconnection hub, powering the Malaysia Internet Exchange (MyIX) with ultra-low latency domestic routing.

Cadangan Strategik: Konsortium Awan Berdaulat Terbuka Malaysia Bersama GLC (TM, TNB, PETRONAS) Strategic Proposal: Malaysian Sovereign Open Cloud Consortium with GLCs (TM, TNB, PETRONAS)

Cadangan Dasar MOTAC MOTAC Policy Blueprint

Bagi membebaskan negara daripada monopoli hyperscaler AS (AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure) serta krisis lonjakan harga pelesenan VMware/Broadcom (300%–1,000%), kerajaan disyorkan menubuhkan sebuah badan amanah industri telus berteraskan ekosistem OpenInfra Foundation (openinfra.org) yang disokong oleh 110,000+ jurutera global. To liberate Malaysia from foreign hyperscaler lock-in (AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure) and the catastrophic VMware/Broadcom licensing cost explosion (300%–1,000%), the government is urged to establish a transparent national consortium anchored on the global OpenInfra Foundation (openinfra.org) ecosystem powered by 110,000+ engineers worldwide.

⚙️ Komponen Perisian Sumber Terbuka OpenInfra (Pengganti AWS, GCP, Azure & VMware) ⚙️ OpenInfra Open-Source Software Components (Replacing AWS, GCP, Azure & VMware)

OpenStack (Nova, Ironic, Neutron, Cinder)
Ganti: AWS EC2/VPC, GCP Compute, Azure VMs, VMware vSphere/VCF Replaces: AWS EC2/VPC, GCP Compute, Azure VMs, VMware vSphere/VCF

Sistem pengendalian awan IaaS penuh. Menghapuskan kenaikan lesen Broadcom RM0, dan dilengkapi enjin Ironic untuk menguruskan kluster cip AI/GPU mentah (bare-metal) pada kelajuan penuh tanpa susut nilai hipervisor. Complete IaaS cloud operating system. Eliminates Broadcom fees entirely (RM0 license), featuring bare-metal Ironic orchestration for maximum GPU cluster performance without hypervisor overhead.

StarlingX (Distributed Edge Cloud)
Ganti: AWS Outposts, Google Distributed Cloud Edge, Azure Stack Replaces: AWS Outposts, Google Distributed Cloud Edge, Azure Stack

Awan pinggir berkependaman rendah (< 1ms) bertaraf telekomunikasi (99.9999% ketersediaan). Sangat ideal untuk automasi grid elektrik TNB, nod 5G O-RAN TM, dan penderia luar pesisir PETRONAS. Carrier-grade ultra-low latency (< 1ms) distributed edge cloud (99.9999% uptime). Purpose-built for TNB power substation automation, TM 5G O-RAN deployments, and PETRONAS offshore telemetry.

Kata Containers (MicroVM Zero-Trust)
Ganti: AWS Firecracker, Google gVisor, VMware Tanzu Isolation Replaces: AWS Firecracker, Google gVisor, VMware Tanzu Isolation

Mengasingkan kontena dalam microVM perkakasan sebenar. Menjamin keselamatan pelbagai kementerian kerajaan dan institusi kewangan berkongsi pelayan awan fizikal yang sama tanpa risiko kebocoran data silang. Encapsulates containers inside hardware-isolated microVMs. Delivers true Zero-Trust isolation so government ministries and financial entities can securely share physical infrastructure.

Ceph Distributed Storage (Objek S3 / Blok)
Ganti: AWS S3 & EBS, Google Cloud Storage, SAN/NAS Proprietari Replaces: AWS S3 & EBS, Google Cloud Storage, Proprietary SAN/NAS

Storan teragih berskala petabyte atas perkakasan komoditi biasa. Tiada caj pemindahan data (zero egress fees), dengan replikasi aktif-aktif merentasi pusat data Lembah Klang dan Johor. Massively scalable petabyte-scale distributed storage on standard commodity servers. Zero data egress penalties, supporting active-active synchronous replication across domestic zones.

Dasar Pengkomputeran Awan Negara (NCCP Ogos 2025) National Cloud Computing Policy (NCCP August 2025)

Kempen ini menyokong visi kerajaan untuk membina awan berdaulat (Sovereign Cloud). Tiga perkara wajib diperjuangkan: (1) Data keselamatan dan maklumat warganegara wajib kekal di pelayan berdaulat; (2) Mengelakkan pengikatan vendor tunggal melalui seni bina awan hibrid berbilang vendor; (3) Memartabatkan piawaian terbuka bagi membolehkan migrasi data secara bebas. This campaign actively champions the government's vision for a genuine Sovereign Cloud. Three imperatives must be upheld: (1) National security and citizen data must strictly reside within sovereign physical servers; (2) Avoid single-vendor lock-in through multi-cloud and hybrid architectures; (3) Enforce open standards to guarantee seamless data portability and auditability.
