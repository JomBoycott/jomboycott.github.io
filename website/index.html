Tindakan boikot dan pembebasan teknologi ini bukan sekadar strategi ekonomi, tetapi kewajipan syarak yang berakar umbi daripada nas-nas sahih menentang penindasan (*az-Zulm*). This tech accountability initiative is not merely an economic boycott, but a profound religious obligation to oppose systemic oppression (az-Zulm).

📖 2 Firman Allah SWT Mengenai Larangan Bersubahat & Cenderung Kepada Orang Zalim 2 Qur'anic Decrees on Forbidding Complicity & Inclining Toward Oppressors

وَتَعَاوَنُوا عَلَى الْبِرِّ وَالتَّقْوَىٰ ۖ وَلَا تَعَاوَنُوا عَلَى الْإِثْمِ وَالْعُدْوَانِ
Surah Al-Ma'idah (5:2) Larangan Bersubahat

"Dan hendaklah kamu bertolong-tolongan untuk membuat kebajikan dan bertakwa, dan janganlah kamu bertolong-tolongan pada melakukan dosa dan pencerobohan (al-'udwan)."

"And cooperate in righteousness and piety, but do not cooperate in sin and aggression."

Tafsir Al-Qurtubi: Membekalkan alat atau infrastruktur yang diketahui akan digunakan untuk kezaliman adalah haram dan bersubahat secara langsung. Tafsir Al-Qurtubi: Providing infrastructure known to facilitate aggression is strictly prohibited.

وَلَا تَرْكَنُوا إِلَى الَّذِينَ ظَلَمُوا فَتَمَسَّكُمُ النَّارُ
Surah Hud (11:113) Larangan Cenderung

"Dan janganlah kamu cenderung (bergantung atau reda) kepada orang-orang yang zalim, yang menyebabkan kamu disentuh api neraka, dan tiadalah bagi kamu selain Allah sebarang penolong..."

"And do not incline toward those who do wrong, lest you be touched by the Fire, and you would have no protectors other than Allah."

Tafsir Ibn Kathir: Melarang daripada menunjukkan sokongan, reda, atau kebergantungan kepada pelaku kezaliman. Tafsir Ibn Kathir: Forbids leaning upon, relying on, or aiding perpetrators of oppression.

📜 3 Sabda Rasulullah ﷺ Mengenai Membanteras Kezaliman 3 Prophetic Hadiths on Eradicating Oppression

«انْصُرْ أَخَاكَ ظَالِمًا أَوْ مَظْلُومًا... تَحْجُزُهُ مِنَ الظُّلْمِ فَإِنَّ ذَلِكَ نَصْرُهُ»
1. Sahih al-Bukhari (No. 2444)

"Tolonglah saudaramu, sama ada dia zalim atau dizalimi." Sahabat bertanya: "Bagaimana menolongnya jika dia zalim?" Baginda bersabda: "Kamu menghalangnya daripada melakukan kezaliman, sesungguhnya demikian itulah cara menolongnya."

"Help your brother, whether oppressor or oppressed." A companion asked how to help the oppressor. The Prophet ﷺ replied: "By restraining him from committing oppression, for that is helping him."

Pengajaran: Menolak kontrak Big Tech adalah tindakan praktikal menyekat instrumen kezaliman. Moral Lesson: Rejecting Big Tech contracts is a practical act of restraining the instruments of oppression.
«يَا عِبَادِي إِنِّي حَرَّمْتُ الظُّلْمَ عَلَى نَفْسِي، وَجَعَلْتُهُ بَيْنَكُمْ مُحَرَّمًا، فَلَا تَظَالَمُوا»
2. Sahih Muslim (No. 2577 — Hadis Qudsi)

Firman Allah SWT dalam Hadis Qudsi: "Wahai hamba-hamba-Ku! Sesungguhnya Aku telah mengharamkan kezaliman ke atas diri-Ku, dan Aku menjadikannya haram di antara kamu, maka janganlah kamu saling menzalimi!"

Allah Almighty decrees in a Hadith Qudsi: "O My servants! I have forbidden oppression for Myself, and I have made it forbidden among you, so do not oppress one another!"

Pengajaran: Pengharaman kezaliman adalah mutlak dan tiada kompromi demi keuntungan korporat. Moral Lesson: The prohibition of oppression is absolute, brooking no compromise for corporate profits.
«اتَّقُوا الظُّلْمَ، فَإِنَّ الظُّلْمَ ظُلُمَاتٌ يَوْMَ الْقِيَامَةِ، وَاتَّقِ دَعْوَةَ الْمَظْلُومِ»
3. Sahih Bukhari (No. 2447) & Muslim (No. 2584)

Sabda Nabi ﷺ: "Jauhilah kezaliman, kerana sesungguhnya kezaliman adalah kegelapan bertindih-tindih pada hari kiamat. Dan takutlah kamu akan doa orang yang dizalimi, kerana tiada hijab antaranya dengan Allah!"

The Prophet ﷺ said: "Beware of oppression, for oppression will be darkness on the Day of Resurrection. And beware of the supplication of the oppressed, for there is no barrier between it and Allah!"

Pengajaran: Kezaliman teknologi moden mengundang hisab dahsyat; doa orang tertindas pasti dimakbulkan. Moral Lesson: Modern technological oppression invites severe reckoning; the supplication of the oppressed is answered.

🔄 Mekanisme Jerangkap Saluran 4-Peringkat Big Tech The 4-Stage Mechanism of Big Tech's Educational Pipeline

Peringkat 1

Umpan Sifar Kos Zero-Cost Ingress

Lesen percuma (DELIMa, Office 365 A1, AWS credits) ditawarkan kepada kementerian bagi menghapuskan saingan FOSS tempatan. Free tiers offered to cash-strapped ministries, crowding out domestic open-source alternatives.

Peringkat 2

Pembiasaan Kognitif Cognitive Lock-In

Murid umur 7 tahun dibiasakan dengan format proprietari; 420K guru dilatih sebagai jurujual tanpa gaji (GCE & MIEE). Students habituated from age 7; 420K teachers become unsalaried brand champions via GCE/MIEE badges.

Peringkat 3

Bait-and-Switch Rent Extraction

Setelah pelayan tempatan ditutup, vendor menarik balik kuota percuma, memaksa universiti membayar lesen bernilai jutaan Ringgit. Once on-premise servers close, vendors slash quotas, forcing universities to buy millions in paid licenses.

Peringkat 4

Penuaian Korporat Enterprise Harvest

Graduan menuntut alatan Big Tech di GLC dan agensi kerajaan, menjamin pengaliran modal berterusan ke Silicon Valley. Graduates demand Big Tech inside GLCs and civil service, securing perpetual capital outflows to Silicon Valley.

Konsortium Awan Berdaulat Terbuka Malaysia: Membina Enjin Data Nasional Bersama GLC (TM, TNB, PETRONAS) Malaysian Sovereign Open Cloud Consortium: Building the National Data Engine with GLCs (TM, TNB, PETRONAS)

Bagaimanakah Malaysia boleh menamatkan monopoli AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure, dan lonjakan harga pelesenan VMware/Broadcom? Dengan menyatukan aset fizikal TM, TNB, dan PETRONAS di bawah ekosistem sumber terbuka global OpenInfra Foundation (openinfra.org). How can Malaysia liberate itself from AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure, and the VMware/Broadcom price explosion? By uniting the physical assets of TM, TNB, and PETRONAS under the global open-source ecosystem of the OpenInfra Foundation (openinfra.org).

⚙️ 4 Komponen Perisian Teras OpenInfra: Pengganti Langsung AWS, GCP, Azure & VMware ⚙️ 4 Core OpenInfra Software Components: Direct Replacements for AWS, GCP, Azure & VMware

OpenStack
Ganti: AWS EC2/VPC, GCP Compute, Azure VMs, VMware vSphere/VCF Replaces: AWS EC2/VPC, GCP Compute, Azure VMs, VMware vSphere/VCF

Sistem pengendalian awan IaaS penuh (45M+ teras CPU global). Modul Ironic orkestrasi pelayan AI/GPU mentah (bare-metal) tanpa susut nilai hipervisor. RM0 royalti lesen. Full IaaS cloud OS (45M+ cores globally). Features bare-metal Ironic orchestration for raw AI GPU clusters without hypervisor overhead. RM0 license fees.

StarlingX
Ganti: AWS Outposts, Google Distributed Edge, Azure Stack Replaces: AWS Outposts, Google Distributed Edge, Azure Stack

Awan pinggir (edge cloud) berkependaman < 1ms bertaraf telekomunikasi (99.9999% ketersediaan). Menjana automasi grid elektrik TNB, 5G O-RAN TM, dan penderia loji PETRONAS. Carrier-grade ultra-low latency (< 1ms) distributed edge cloud. Powers TNB smart power grid automation, TM 5G O-RAN, and PETRONAS industrial sensors.

Kata Containers
Ganti: AWS Firecracker, Google gVisor, Tanzu Isolation Replaces: AWS Firecracker, Google gVisor, Tanzu Isolation

Pengasingan keselamatan Zero-Trust menggunakan microVM perkakasan sebenar. Pelbagai kementerian berkongsi pelayan awan fizikal tanpa risiko pencerobohan data silang. Hardware-isolated microVM Zero-Trust security. Multiple ministries share physical infrastructure without cross-tenant memory snooping or leakage.

Ceph Storage
Ganti: AWS S3 & EBS, Google Cloud Storage, SAN Dell/EMC Replaces: AWS S3 & EBS, Google Cloud Storage, Proprietary SAN

Storan teragih berskala exabyte atas pelayan komoditi. Sifar caj pemindahan (zero egress fees) dengan replikasi data aktif-aktif merentasi pusat data domestik. Massively scalable exabyte-scale distributed storage on standard servers. Zero egress penalty with active-active synchronous replication across domestic zones.

Model Penyatuan Dana (Pooled Sovereign Funding) Pooled Sovereign Funding Model

Setiap tahun anggaran RM2–RM4 bilion wang awam dan GLC mengalir ke luar negara dalam bentuk lesen perisian proprietari dan yuran hyperscaler USD. Mengalihkan 15%–20% ke dalam Tabung Infrastruktur Awan Terbuka Kebangsaan (Ringgit Malaysia) mampu menjimatkan 40%–60% kos IT negara dan membiayai pelayan domestik secara mampan. Every year, an estimated RM2–RM4 billion in public and GLC funds leaks abroad in USD licensing and hyperscaler fees. Redirecting 15%–20% into a domestic National Open Cloud Infrastructure Fund (in MYR) saves 40%–60% in operational costs and sustainably finances sovereign hardware.

🎓

Enjin Bakat Tempatan (Local Talent Engine) Local Talent Engine & Upstream Academy

Transforming the IT workforce from 'foreign software consumers' to upstream open-source system builders and kernel architects. Launching the National Open Cloud Engineering Academy (COA, CKA certifications) and sponsoring 100 top local engineers as global upstream contributors.
