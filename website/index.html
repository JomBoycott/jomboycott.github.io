Dua Tunjang Perjuangan Kempen Two Pillars of the Campaign

Bukan sekadar boikot emosi; kami menyatukan akauntabiliti moral antarabangsa dengan pembangunan kedaulatan teknologi tempatan. Beyond emotional boycotts; unifying international moral accountability with domestic technological independence.

⚖️

Tunjang 1: Akauntabiliti Teknologi & Palestin Pillar 1: Tech Complicity & Palestine

Mendokumentasikan kontrak awan pertahanan, pemprosesan data ketenteraan berasaskan AI, dan penapisan ucapan media sosial secara telus. Berpegang kepada prinsip 'Jangan mereka kepastian' bagi mempertahankan kredibiliti. Documenting defense cloud tenders, military AI pipelines, and platform content censorship with rigorous evidentiary standards.

Tunjang 2: Kedaulatan Digital Malaysia Pillar 2: Malaysian Digital Sovereignty

Menamatkan kebergantungan melampau kepada monopoli 4 gergasi asing. Memperkasa pusat data domestik (TM One, AIMS), perisian sumber terbuka (Nextcloud, Matrix, Linux), dan melindungi data negara daripada Akta CLOUD AS. Ending excessive dependency on foreign monopolies. Empowering domestic datacenters and open-source stacks under the National Cloud Computing Policy.

4 Syarikat Gergasi Diteliti 4 Tech Giants Examined

Penilaian berasaskan bukti dokumen rasmi kerajaan, bukan andaian. Evaluated based on official government documentation, not rumors.

Nimbus $1.2B

Google Cloud

Pemenang bersama Project Nimbus. Kementerian Pertahanan Israel disahkan mempunyai saluran GCP khusus. 50+ pekerja dipecat. Co-winner of Nimbus. Israel Ministry of Defense confirmed to have dedicated GCP landing zone. 50+ staff fired.

Awan $7.2B

Amazon AWS

Tulang belakang komputasi awan Israel. Melabur $7.2B di Tel Aviv. Terikat syarat tender untuk tidak menyekat tentera. Primary compute backbone. $7.2B investment in Tel Aviv region. Contractually barred from withholding services.

Sekatan 2025

Microsoft Azure

Membekalkan sistem pertahanan, namun pada Sept 2025 menamatkan akses tentera kepada teknologi pengawasan tertentu. Supplies defense organs, yet in Sept 2025 terminated military access to specific mass surveillance tools.

Audit BSR & HRW

Meta Platforms

Bukan awan tentera, tetapi pengawal maklumat. Audit mengesahkan penapisan sistematik terhadap kandungan Palestin. Not military cloud, but gatekeeper. Independent audits confirm systemic censorship of Palestinian voices.
