# 1. IMPORTANT RESEARCH RULE

## DO NOT manufacture certainty.

The campaign has a clear ethical position.

The research must nevertheless distinguish between:

### A. Verified fact

Supported directly by:

* official government documents
* court documents
* ICC documents
* ICJ documents
* UN documents
* company filings
* procurement documents
* contracts
* official company statements
* official military documents
* parliamentary records
* reputable investigative journalism

### B. Strongly supported reporting

Supported by multiple reputable independent sources.

### C. Allegation

A credible organisation or journalist makes the allegation, but the underlying facts remain disputed or incomplete.

### D. Interpretation

An analysis derived from established facts.

### E. Campaign opinion

The campaign's ethical or political conclusion.

NEVER convert B, C, D or E into A.

# 9. CLAIM DISCIPLINE

Never write:

unless the exact claim and legal basis can be supported by authoritative evidence.

> "Investigations and documented contracts show that Google provides cloud infrastructure to the Israeli government under Project Nimbus. Researchers and human-rights organisations have raised concerns about how such infrastructure may support state and military operations."

The campaign can make an ethical conclusion:

> "We believe Malaysians should not be dependent on technology companies whose services may contribute to systems of oppression."
