# Amazon / AWS: Research Dossier

**Entity:** Amazon.com, Inc. / Amazon Web Services (AWS) (Seattle, Washington, USA)  
**Primary Services Examined:** AWS Cloud Infrastructure, Amazon SageMaker, AWS Rekognition, AWS Tel Aviv Region (`il-central-1`), Project Nimbus  
**Key Documented Partner/Client:** Israeli Ministry of Finance, Israeli Ministry of Defense, Israeli Defense Forces (IDF), Israel Police  
**Status:** High Complicity (Primary Cloud Infrastructure & Compute Provider)

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Amazon Web Services (AWS) is the world's largest cloud provider and a co-winner of the Israeli government's \$1.2 billion **Project Nimbus** alongside Google Cloud. In addition to the shared Nimbus umbrella, AWS has made one of the largest foreign direct investments in Israel's history by launching the **AWS Tel Aviv Region (`il-central-1`)** in August 2023, committing an estimated \$7.2 billion through 2037.

AWS serves as the backbone for Israeli state digital infrastructure, hosting mission-critical government, public safety, and defense applications. The contract binds AWS to provide continuous, uninterrupted compute power to all government departments without the legal or contractual ability to withhold services from the military or security agencies.

---

## 2. Documented Infrastructure & Regional Operations

### 2.1 Project Nimbus Allocation
* **Procurement:** Awarded in April 2021 by the Ministry of Finance tender committee.
* **Role:** AWS provides large-scale infrastructure-as-a-service (IaaS), high-performance computing (HPC), relational and NoSQL databases, object storage (S3), and machine learning pipelines (Amazon SageMaker).
* **Guaranteed Service Continuity:** The terms of the tender require AWS to operate through local Israeli subsidiaries, ensuring that international legal challenges, sanctions, or corporate embargoes cannot legally halt the provision of services to the Israeli state.

### 2.2 AWS Israel (Tel Aviv) Region (`il-central-1`)
* **Launch Date:** August 1, 2023.
* **Scale:** 3 Availability Zones located inside Israel.
* **Investment Commitment:** \$7.2 Billion over a 15-year horizon.
* **Economic & Strategic Impact:** Allows Israeli public sector entities, financial institutions, and military units to run high-throughput workloads with sub-millisecond latency while ensuring complete local data residency, insulating Israeli state operations from foreign data disclosure orders.

## 3. Defense, Police & Security Relationships

### 3.1 Documented Defense & Law Enforcement Usage
* **Ministry of Defense Integration:** Official tender reports confirm that AWS is approved for use across the entire defense apparatus under the Nimbus framework.
* **Police & Intelligence:** Israel Police and state surveillance branches utilize AWS infrastructure for biometric storage, automated license plate recognition (ALPR), and analytical databases.
* **Unit 8200 & Military Intelligence:** Investigative reporting by *+972 Magazine* and *The Guardian* on military data pipelines indicated that massive intelligence data lakes, intercepted phone records, and surveillance video feeds from Gaza and the West Bank rely upon scalable commercial cloud infrastructure provided under government framework agreements.

### 3.2 Advanced Machine Learning Tooling (AWS Suite)
* **Amazon SageMaker:** Used by enterprise and government clients to build, train, and deploy machine learning models at scale.
* **AWS Rekognition:** Computer vision API providing automated object, face, text, and scene detection, frequently cited by civil liberties organisations for potential surveillance abuse in occupied territories.

## 4. Retail & Settlement Logistics Controversy

Beyond cloud computing, Amazon has faced scrutiny regarding commercial retail policies in the occupied West Bank:
* In 2020, investigations revealed that Amazon offered free shipping to illegal Israeli settlements in the West Bank while charging Palestinian residents in nearby towns high delivery fees unless they listed their country as "Israel".
* Following sustained international civil rights pressure and threats of legal action, Amazon updated its shipping policy to extend free delivery to Palestinian addresses.

## 5. Employee Resistance & BDS Focus

### 5.1 Internal Dissent
* Along with Google employees, hundreds of Amazon corporate and tech workers published open letters condemned Project Nimbus in the *Guardian* (October 2021): *"We cannot look the other way, as the products we build are used to deny Palestinians their basic rights."*
* Amazon workers have introduced shareholder resolutions demanding independent human rights due diligence on AWS military contracts; however, Amazon's board of directors has consistently recommended voting against these resolutions.

### 5.2 BDS Campaign Priority
The Palestinian-led Boycott, Divestment, Sanctions (BDS) National Committee officially categorised Amazon (alongside Google) as a **primary target** under its tech boycott guide due to its direct role in Project Nimbus.

## 7. Malaysian Dimension

* In August 2024, AWS launched the **AWS Asia Pacific (Malaysia) Region (`ap-southeast-5`)** with a planned \$6.2 billion investment through 2038.
* The Malaysian government, via MAMPU and the Ministry of Digital, signed the Cloud Framework Agreement (CFA) with AWS to power public sector digital transformation.
* This dynamic demonstrates how Malaysian public finances directly contribute to AWS revenue while national data systems become locked into the same proprietary ecosystem that powers Israel's defense cloud.
